# Beacon analytics: move to PostHog

Date: 2026-10-10
Author: Alex Rivera
Approved by: Dana Ortiz

## Decision

Beacon will replace Mixpanel with PostHog for product analytics, effective November 1, 2026. Mixpanel stays the system of record until then.

## Rationale

PostHog can be self-hosted in the EU region, which the data residency review requires.
